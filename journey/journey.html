What We Built

Instant Reporting

GPS detection, photo upload, 30-second delay reports from your phone.

Community Verification

Upvote real delays. Auto-verify at 3 votes. Collective intelligence wins.

Points & Rewards

8+ points per verified report. Redeem for transit passes, coffee, entertainment.

Dynamic Routing

C++ engine calculates alternate paths in <10ms around verified delays.

Mobile-First

44-64px touch targets. Bottom nav. One-handed. Works on 3G.

Enterprise-Ready

Resilient architecture. Graceful degradation. Production-tested reliability.

Three-Layer Architecture

Frontend: Next.js 15 + React

12 pages, 50+ components, mobile-first responsive

Middleware: FastAPI + Python

20+ endpoints, points engine, auto-verification

Core: C++17 Routing Engine

<10ms routes, Dijkstra, thread-safe, SSE streaming
